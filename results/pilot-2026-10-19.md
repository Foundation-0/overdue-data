# Pilot results, 19 October 2026

**Status: pilot, claimed, not peer reviewed.**

Counts only. The per-dataset results name papers and accessions, so they stay out of this repository (see the
policy in [README.md](../README.md)). An accession is the ID an archive gives a dataset: PXD and six digits at
ProteomeXchange, GSE and digits at GEO.

The papers were first published between January and March 2025. On 19 October 2026 that is 18 to 21 months after
publication.

## ProteomeXchange

Script: [pilot/pilot_fulltext.py](../pilot/pilot_fulltext.py). The sample is the first 500 papers that Europe PMC
returned in a search for open-access papers that mention "ProteomeXchange" or "PRIDE partner repository". On 19
October 2026, 1,131 papers matched that search.

| Count | Value |
|---|---|
| Papers read | 500 |
| Papers with at least one PXD accession in the full text | 490 |
| Distinct PXD accessions | 694 |
| Public | 684 |
| Not yet released | 10 |
| Papers that cite an unreleased dataset | 10 (2% of 500) |

The unreleased datasets by archive: PRIDE 4, MassIVE 4, iProX 2. ProteomeXchange is a group of archives, and each
dataset is held by one of them.

Checked again while setting up this repository, on the evening of 19 October 2026:

- All 10 datasets were still unreleased, with the same split by archive.
- In each of the 10 papers, the accession sits in a sentence saying the data were deposited in, submitted to or
  are available from ProteomeXchange. Three of these papers were also read by hand during the pilot.
- In 2 of the 10 papers, the paper names PRIDE as the archive, but ProteomeXchange says MassIVE holds the dataset.
  These two need a closer look before any report, in case the accession in the paper is mistyped.

## GEO

Script: [pilot/pilot_fulltext_geo.py](../pilot/pilot_fulltext_geo.py). The sample is the first 500 papers that Europe PMC
returned in a search for open-access papers that say their data were deposited in GEO. On 19 October 2026, 859
papers matched that search. GEO calls a dataset a series.

| Count | Value |
|---|---|
| Papers read | 500 |
| Papers with at least one GSE accession in the full text | 493 |
| Distinct GSE accessions | 987 |
| Public | 985 |
| Private | 1, with release scheduled for 20 February 2029 |
| Deleted by GEO staff | 1, in December 2024 |

The script recorded the deleted series as "other". A check by hand during setup found GEO's page saying that GEO
staff had deleted it. The private series was checked again during setup and still says it is private.

## Europe PMC's annotations miss unreleased datasets

Europe PMC marks up accession numbers it finds in papers. Earlier runs took accessions from those annotations
instead of from the full text, for papers from the same three months:

| Archive | Accessions from annotations | Papers with an annotated accession | Public |
|---|---|---|---|
| GEO | 1,291 | 594 | 1,291 |
| ProteomeXchange | 610 | 425 | 610 |

Every one was public, so the annotations seem to keep only accessions of public datasets. Accessions must come
from the full text. [pilot/pilot_pxd.py](../pilot/pilot_pxd.py) is kept to show this.

## How a status was read

- ProteomeXchange: the PROXI API at `https://proteomecentral.proteomexchange.org/api/proxi/v0.1/datasets/<accession>`
  answers `DatasetNotYetReleased` for a dataset that is not yet public, and names the archive that holds it.
- GEO: the page `https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=<accession>&targ=self&form=text&view=brief` says
  a private series is "currently private" and gives its scheduled release date.

## Limits

- The papers are the first 500 in Europe PMC's order, not a random sample.
- Only open-access papers whose full text is in Europe PMC are covered.
- Some cases will be typos in the paper or withdrawn papers. That rate has not been measured yet.
- Ten cases is a small number, so the 2% is rough: a 95% confidence interval runs from 1.1% to 3.6%.
