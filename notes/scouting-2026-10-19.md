# Scouting notes, 18–19 October 2026

This is the record of the work done before the repository was set up: Foundation0's scouting of 18–19 October 2026
([deep-fields](https://github.com/Foundation-0/f0/tree/main/deep-fields)) and a pilot run on 19 October 2026.

## The question

Which datasets that published papers say were deposited in a public archive are still unreleased more than 18 months
later?

Many papers say "the data have been deposited" in an archive such as ProteomeXchange (protein mass spectrometry) or
GEO (gene expression), with an accession number. Sometimes the data stay private long after the paper is out, so
nobody can check or reuse them. Archives release a dataset when told it has been published. This project finds
those datasets so the archives can follow them up.

## Policy

- Publish counts and the share of datasets released after follow-up. Do not publish a list naming papers or authors.
- Send per-dataset lists privately to the archive's curators, and only after a person signs off.
- Every case is a question for the authors, never an accusation. Some will be typos in the paper.

## Checked on 19 Oct 2026

### Earlier work

- Wide-Open: Grechkin, Poon and Howe, "Wide-Open: Accelerating public data release by automating detection of
  overdue datasets", PLOS Biology 2017
  ([article](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pbio.2002477)). It identified 473
  overdue GEO datasets in February 2017 and reported them to GEO. About 400 were released within the first week,
  and 455 by the time the article was published. It also found 84 overdue datasets in SRA, NCBI's archive of raw
  sequencing reads, by March 2017. The scouting report gave the GEO numbers. They were checked against the article
  during setup.
- Valentine Svensson, "GEO data unlocks vs paper publication", blog, 1 Jul 2026
  ([post](https://www.nxn.se/p/geo-data-unlocks-vs-paper-publication)): "There is a tail of 18% of datasets that
  aren't released for more than a month past the publication of the paper." The post does not give its sample size.
  Checked against the post during setup.

### The pilot

The scripts are in [pilot/](../pilot/) and the counts in
[results/pilot-2026-10-19.md](../results/pilot-2026-10-19.md). Counts were confirmed from the saved outputs during
setup.

- [pilot_fulltext.py](../pilot/pilot_fulltext.py) takes ProteomeXchange (PXD) accessions from the full text of
  open-access papers (Europe PMC's fullTextXML) and checks them against the PROXI API.
  - 500 open-access papers from January to March 2025 that mention ProteomeXchange gave 694 PXD accessions.
  - 10 are still unreleased, in 10 papers (2%): 4 at PRIDE, 4 at MassIVE, 2 at iProX.
  - In three of the papers, read by hand, each says "data have been deposited ... with the dataset identifier
    PXD...".
- [pilot_fulltext_geo.py](../pilot/pilot_fulltext_geo.py) does the same for GEO (GSE) accessions, checked with
  GEO's acc.cgi text form. GEO has nearly solved the problem: 500 papers from the same months gave 987 GSE
  accessions, and 1 is still private, with release scheduled for 20 February 2029.
- Pitfall: Europe PMC's accession annotations seem to keep only accessions that resolve publicly. Of 1,291 GSE and
  610 PXD accessions taken from annotations, every one was public. Accessions must come from the full text.
  [pilot_pxd.py](../pilot/pilot_pxd.py) is one of the annotation-based versions, kept to document this. The others
  were dropped: one for GEO, which first marked every series as not found because its status check was wrong, and
  the script that re-checked those series with GEO's text form.

### How anyone checks a case

- ProteomeXchange: the PROXI API returns `DatasetNotYetReleased` for a dataset that is not yet public. The address
  is `https://proteomecentral.proteomexchange.org/api/proxi/v0.1/datasets/<accession>`. The answer also names the
  archive that holds the dataset.
- GEO: the page for a private series says it is "currently private", and asks to be told if it has been used in a
  publication.

### Checks made during setup, evening of 19 Oct 2026

- All 10 unreleased PXD datasets were queried again. All were still unreleased: 4 at PRIDE, 4 at MassIVE, 2 at
  iProX.
- The full text of the 10 papers was read automatically around each accession. In all 10, the accession sits in a
  sentence saying the data were deposited in (8), submitted to (1) or are available from (1) ProteomeXchange.
- In 2 of the 10, the paper names PRIDE, but ProteomeXchange says MassIVE holds the dataset. These need a closer
  look before any report.
- The GEO output had one series marked "other" besides the private one. GEO's page says GEO staff deleted it in
  December 2024. The pilot's first summary counted only the private series.
- The private GEO series still says it is private, with release on 20 February 2029.
- Both full-text scripts ran on a sample of 3 papers. Europe PMC's searches matched 1,131 papers (ProteomeXchange)
  and 859 papers (GEO) on that day, so the pilot read the first 500 of each in Europe PMC's order, not a random
  sample.
